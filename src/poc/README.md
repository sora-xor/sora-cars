# Garage shader proof

Run `npm run dev -- --host 127.0.0.1` and open `/visual-poc.html`.

This is the small, isolated 3D proof requested before further game-wide visual work. The reference is `public/assets/story/angles/garage-wide-v2.png`. The comparison button displays that image explicitly as a reference; it is never used to replace the 3D scene.

The **Painted shading** toggle controls the custom shader treatments:

- `painted-surfaces.ts` keeps the detailed material textures, physical lights and shadows, then groups diffuse light into irregular pigment patches. Warm/cool underpainting breaks broad panels; the same brush field interrupts rounded metal highlights while preserving their light direction. Brush coordinates follow mesh surfaces at physical scale. Derivative filtering suppresses unresolved fine marks. Transparent and transmissive materials keep their source shading.
- `painted-bodywork.ts` gives the car enamel a separate treatment: nearly clean albedo, restrained grouping of diffuse values, and larger attached strokes on the actual specular and clearcoat lobes. Triplanar directions follow the hood, flanks and ends of the car. Derivative filtering uses the perturbed lighting threshold, and unresolved brush octaves fade out. `garagePOC.paintedSurfaces.setBodyworkStrength(0…1)` compares it with the previous generic surface treatment.
- The source shaders also apply a broader illustrated-light variant: stronger, fewer diffuse value regions, cool shadow pigment and cream-lit planes. Existing large surface-attached strokes break the transition boundaries; material textures and reflected highlights retain their detail. Gain and low-energy limits restrain the response, and unresolved bands fade. `garagePOC.paintedSurfaces.setIllustrationStrength(0…1)` compares the earlier, smoother lighting with this variant. Metallic engine materials only increase their existing diffuse grouping strength; their separate specular treatment stays the same. The floor and foliage retain their own material-specific shaders.
- `painted-edges.ts` adds sparse source-light catches to rounded workshop edges. A conditioned tangent metric estimates convex curvature from view-space geometry and pre-normal-map normals; confidence and pixel resolution scale the final mark. Attached pigment breaks it into small accents. Primary car enamel keeps its separate treatment. `garagePOC.paintedSurfaces.setEdgeStrength(0…1)` isolates these catches.
- `painted-engine.ts` treats the foreground engine's metallic batches separately. Warped, varied triplanar strokes change roughness and shallow casting relief before lighting, then group actual reflected radiance into restrained paint shapes. Low-light gating and bounded gain keep dark cavities from acquiring bright specks. Rubber, wiring, enamel and lettering retain their previous treatment. `garagePOC.paintedSurfaces.setEngineStrength(0…1)` compares this with the generic surface treatment.
- `garage-glazing.ts` gives the actual car windows thin-sheet optics. It removes diffuse haze, retains physical direct/environment reflections independently of alpha coverage, and uses Fresnel plus neutral absorption to reveal the existing interior. A restrained reflection-paint treatment follows the same real lighting. Glazing receives car/garage shadows. `garagePOC.glazing.setEnabled(false/true)` isolates the optics; Painted shading controls its reflected paint shapes.
- `cutscene-shader.ts` groups nearby colors with an original bounded sector painter filter: 32 ellipse samples, eight continuously weighted color-variance sectors and a smoothed color structure tensor. Depth-slope rejection prevents blending across geometry; a minmod slope protects thin cables surrounded by background. Strong color residuals preserve labels, seams and narrow highlights. Weakly directional texture receives nearly equal sector weights to avoid amplifying unstable orientation. Colored shadow pigments, value steps and selected depth creases follow the filtering, after one tone/color conversion.
- `painted-floor.ts` projects a detailed concrete albedo onto the real floor, with subdued slab cracks and localized dampness. Its own light shader groups actual diffuse illumination using the authored pigment as a threshold phase; derivative filtering, a low-energy gate and bounded gain restrain bright specks. The floor skips the generic surface paint pass to avoid stacking unrelated light bands. All marks are world anchored and are evaluated in the reflected camera as well. `garagePOC.paintedFloor.setStrength(0…1)` isolates the source concrete treatment; `setLightPaintStrength(0…1)` isolates the new diffuse grouping while retaining the chip catches.
- The floor reflection shader in `garage.ts` breaks the actual mirrored geometry into surface-anchored marks, using the existing normal and roughness scans. It shares the source concrete's world-space dampness field, so dry areas do not carry unrelated mirror patches.

`garage-soft-shadows.ts` replaces the local standard/physical-material PCF function with a fixed, tapered 32-sample disk in shadow-map axes. It composes existing material hooks and checks the installed Three implementation before replacing it; global shader chunks stay untouched. This provides fixed-radius softness, not area-light contact hardening. `garagePOC.softShadows.setStrength(0…1)` compares a one-texel disk with the configured light radius, using 32 samples in both states. The lighting places a stronger warm pendant over the main car and reduces general front fill. This shadow treatment stays enabled in the user-facing Painted shading comparison.

`garage-car-reflections.ts` captures one 512px linear HDR workshop probe at each parked car and filters it for physical roughness. Cars and the planar floor mirror are excluded during capture; scene/render state is restored and shadow maps invalidated for the next ordinary frame. These actual local reflections feed the bodywork and windows before painting. `garagePOC.localReflections.setEnabled(false/true)` isolates them. These are static probes for stationary POC cars, without parallax correction or updates for a moving race.

The enamel uses a `.85` clearcoat with `.11` coat roughness over the existing `.25` base roughness. This resolves more of the real workshop fixtures on the hood while retaining dielectric paint rather than a metallic mirror.

Diagnostic floor/shadow strength and albedo changes affect live receivers; they do not regenerate these static car probes.

`workshop-concrete.ts` loads the generated `public/assets/materials/painted-workshop/concrete-v1.png` as sRGB albedo at a 5.2 m repeat scale. Four offset samples crossfade near texture borders, using continuous world derivatives for mip selection. The 1254² image is copied unchanged, with its exact prompt and provenance alongside it. The authored map receives no additional broad scan blur. `garagePOC.paintedFloor.setAuthoredStrength(0…1)` compares it with the earlier scan foundation, whose minimum 1.8 cm footprint is retained for diagnostics. `setAuthoredScale(metres)` changes the repeat size. The authored floor albedo remains present when the user turns Painted shading off. Scanned AO and some normal response remain; seam crossfades can blend nearby marks and recognizable larger stains can repeat.

The same capture helper also supplies a separate engine probe at world position `(3.7, 1.504, 3.75)`. It excludes the engine, both cars and the planar mirror, and feeds only the five metallic engine batches. `garagePOC.engineReflections.setEnabled(false/true)` isolates that capture while preserving car probes. Its stationary-scene and parallax limitations are the same as the parked-car probes.

Portrait framing preserves at least 50° horizontal coverage, so the full main car fits the proof; landscape remains at its previous 45° vertical field of view.

`glazing-mask.ts` draws glass geometry into a separate single-channel target against a copy of opaque depth. The post shader reduces seat/interior ink inside visible windows without changing beauty depth or transparent ordering. `garagePOC.pass.setGlazingMaskEnabled(false/true)` isolates that suppression. Disable both this mask and `glazing.setEnabled` when comparing the complete earlier window appearance. The thin-sheet optics preserve existing geometry and do not model volumetric refraction.

The independent **Pixel finish** toggle samples the fully shaded image on a fine final grid. Drag to turn the camera, scroll to zoom, use Orbit for continuous movement, or Reset view to return to the starting composition.

The sector approach is informed by [Kyprianidis, Kang and Döllner, *Anisotropic Kuwahara Filtering on the GPU*](https://www.kyprianidis.com/p/gpupro/). The POC implementation is original, with a small fixed sample budget, scene-depth constraints and detail preservation for this live 3D scene. `garagePOC.pass.setPainterStrength(0…1)` isolates this additional filter for diagnostics while retaining the other painted treatments. The user-facing Painted shading switch controls the combined finish.

The source pass adds a generated illustrated steel albedo (`public/assets/materials/painted-workshop/steel-v1.png`) on the real garage walls/ceiling. `workshop-steel.ts` projects it at a consistent metre scale and crossfades offset repeats. `painted-headlamps.ts` normalizes coordinates to the actual paired lens geometry, then shades reflector facets, prism lanes, dark dividers and warm emission; diffuse and reflected light preserve that structure. `painted-sky.ts` creates direction-anchored clustered dusk clouds with smaller silhouette marks, interior value patches and selective pink lower rims. `garage-horizon.ts` adds four actual 3D terrain layers, shallow branching Fuji ridges, a visible painted lake and batched shoreline settlements. Distant ink fades with distance to avoid tracing terrain facets. The shader switch includes the procedural sky, while the authored material and geometry remain present in both comparison states.

`shelf-contents.ts` replaces the placeholder objects with four distinct arrangements of labeled tins, hollow-handle jugs, taped cartons, manuals, folded cloth, spare parts and a helmet. These are real geometry, merged into 16 material batches (62,660 triangles). `engine-assembly.ts` adds a complete freestanding four-cylinder engine on the foreground trolley, with curved intake/exhaust, belts, pulleys, ignition leads, plumbing and raised original lettering (10 material batches, 49,960 triangles). The two parked cars and inward-facing workbenches provide a focused inspection scene. Warm/cool pigment glazes and diffuse variation stay attached to those surfaces as the camera moves.

The sky renders first without a depth test to keep derivative quads defined along occluding terrain. Its per-camera inverse is derived from the actual projection matrix because the floor reflector changes its camera's oblique projection without updating the cached inverse. Actual frozen on/off/on and repeated-frame checks verify stable restoration.

`garage-shoreline.ts` adds three maple trees between the garage and the lake. Tapered trunks and twigs carry 13,650 instances of a folded, seven-lobed leaf (`shoreline-leaf.ts`, 48 triangles per leaf). The silhouettes are opaque 3D geometry, shared by the beauty, depth, shadow and reflection views. Branch-level colors and crown exposure keep the foliage in dark blue-violet masses. A separate leaf shader adds UV-attached pigment and veins, groups actual diffuse lighting, and fades unresolved detail; it skips the generic paint shader. `garagePOC.shoreline.setPainted(false/true)` isolates that leaf treatment. The user Painted shading toggle also controls it. An irregular bank descends below the lake at its outer edges, and root placement uses that bank's height field. This adds seven mesh objects and 676,380 triangles, bringing the proof to 2,572,877 triangles including instances; it is not a production foliage LOD system.

This proves the shader on live 3D geometry, not cutscene parity. The garage still has less authored surface detail, simpler far terrain and much less overlapping scene detail than the reference. The shader has not been adopted into the production game.

Validation: TypeScript and the Playwright interaction/capture check in `tmp/fidelity/capture-poc.mjs`. The check verifies shader and pixelation changes, restoration after shader on/off/on, drag and auto orbit, reset, reference comparison, and desktop/mobile WebGL errors. Restoration reports byte exactness separately from a narrowly bounded allowance for observed GPU rounding: at most one 8-bit color level across eight pixels at the test viewport. Larger differences still fail. Three fresh-context frozen-frame trials found two isolated mismatches of one and five pixels at that amplitude on engine edges; no renderer-state cause was confirmed. The repository-local develop-web-game client also runs using the Metal/readiness adapter in `tmp/fidelity/poc-skill-preload.mjs`.
