# Midnight Circuit — National Route 1, Hakone-Yumoto to Miyanoshita, summer 1986

Research of 2026-11-11 for the Arcade course's real setting. The course is 6.0 km (three 2 km laps drawn end to
end); course progress `p` is `200 + p` metres along the traced road (`hakone-route-1.road.json`), so the station
front is at p ≈ 20 and the Fujiya Hotel at p ≈ 6,280, in the run-off beyond the finish line. Sides are for uphill
travel. `[O]` is OpenStreetMap geometry, `[G]` a line-of-sight calculation on GSI elevation tiles; both describe
today's terrain. Nothing here is surveyed.

## Mountains [G]

- Yumoto–Tōnosawa: Yusaka-yama's wall left, Tōnomine right, Sengen-yama ahead.
- Deyama–Ōhiradai: Sengen-yama left and ahead, Myōjōgatake right across the gorge.
- Miyanoshita: Myōjōgatake's summit directly right; Sōunzan and Kamiyama ahead-left.
- The 大 on Myōjōgatake (lit 16 August since 1921) faces Gōra; obliquely in sight from Ōhiradai and Miyanoshita.
- **Mt. Fuji is not visible anywhere on this stretch** (the Fujiya's own site says so [17]; by calculation the summit
  never clears the terrain).

## Road and vehicles

- Two undivided lanes; widths fixed by the 1930s structures. No pavements beyond Tōnosawa today.
- Hakone Tozan buses: white with two blues and a red line from 1980 [23]. Tozan trains: orange-vermilion with a grey
  window band (1957 scheme); "Bernina" 1000-series sets from 1981 [26][27].
- Unconfirmed for 1986: guard rails, retaining walls, lighting, signs, Ekiden markers (live television began 1987).

## Not there in 1986

Elevated station (2009) and deck (2012); the Kanrei-dōmon bypass and its bridges (2014); EXIV Hakone Rikyū (2010);
NARAYA CAFE (2007); the Fujiya's Cascade Wing (2020).
