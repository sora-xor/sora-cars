# Roof material source

[Grey Roof Tiles 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/grey_roof_tiles_02), by Rob Tuytel. The original material is [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) under the [Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license).

The original API metadata and download manifest are retained as `source-info.json` and `source-manifest.json`. The local 2K JPEGs are unchanged source bytes: `color.jpg` is `Diffuse.2k.jpg`, `normal.jpg` is `nor_gl.2k.jpg`, and `arm.jpg` is `arm.2k.jpg`. All three byte lengths and MD5 hashes were verified against the manifest on 2026-09-13. `verified-files.json` records official source URLs, sizes and checksums, including SHA-256.

The renderer samples color as sRGB and normal/ARM as linear data, repeats the material at meter scale, and applies it to modeled sloping roofs with real raised seams and ridge geometry. These are generic roof tiles; the source does not establish a specific Japanese period or manufacturer. No downloaded preview images are used.
