# Beige Wall 001

CC0 photographed painted plaster by Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers, from
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/beige_wall_001).

The unchanged official 2048² color, OpenGL (+Y) normal and packed ARM maps use
R=AO, G=roughness, B=metalness. Source dimensions are 3 × 3 metres. Metadata,
URLs, byte sizes and verified MD5/SHA-256 hashes are retained beside the maps.
Color uses sRGB; normal and ARM use linear data. Dielectric metalness is zero.

For the authored pale facade palette, the shader divides the decoded source
color by its measured mean linear RGB [0.339758, 0.262138, 0.188094] and tints
that relative variation with the material paint color, bounded to reflectance
one. These are texture statistics, not independent physical measurements.
Source roughness and normal strength are retained. The older garage wall keeps
Grey Plaster 03 at its official 1 × 1 metre repeat.
