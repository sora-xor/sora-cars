**FRI and Reed–Solomon: distinguish a new analysis from qualified parameters.** The current record for *A Simplified Round-by-round Soundness Proof of FRI* remains a preprint revised **March 31, 2026**. Its version-3 changelog adds stronger security properties and batched FRI; version 2 had corrected the folding-round state function. It is a useful reference for a concrete security worksheet, rather than an automatic reason to lower query counts. [Primary record and changelog](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/1993).

Two recent technical reports remain particularly relevant: TR26-164's **September 5 revision** extends its list-decoding result from low rates to all constant rates over prime fields; TR26-169 was **published September 6**, following a September 5 submission. The latter's abstract requires fixed slack, sufficiently large block length and a prime field, and gives polynomial factors with slack-dependent exponents in its agreement error. These are ECCC reports, not verified conference-publication claims. Neither record shows a September 9 revision. [TR26-164](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/164/), [TR26-169](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/169/).

For Iroha, applying these results requires explicit finite constants, the precise evaluation domain and compatibility with Goldilocks/Fp4 challenges. They do not automatically restore the original conjectures disproved by Crites–Stewart, whose preprint remains revised **December 19, 2025**. Our inference is to keep current parameters while checking the exact replacement premises and all composition losses. [Counterexamples and modified conjectures](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/2046).

**DEEP-ALI is an existing algebraic-linking technique, not a new 2026 backend.** The original DEEP-FRI paper was published at **ITCS on January 6, 2020** and explicitly introduces both DEEP-FRI and DEEP-ALI. The first tests proximity to a code; the second improves the algebraic-linking step used in STARK constructions. Its abstract separates the proved Johnson-regime result from stronger conjectural claims. This distinction matters when assessing whether a commitment experiment proves the complete game relation. [Official proceedings and abstract](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2020.5).

The **August 28 LiftWHIR preprint** combines interleaving and DEEP before using WHIR for the reduced tasks. Its reported 4.4–6.7× evaluation-phase gains become 1.55–1.61× when commitment is included, with larger proofs and more verification work, in an experiment over a 255-bit prime field. These remain author-reported component measurements. They cannot be applied as multipliers to the native complete-history timings. A proposed native experiment must retain scalar, range, ROM, public-port and state-continuity obligations throughout the reduction. [LiftWHIR record](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/1561).

**Hash security needs the exact construction and attack model.** The current *Billion Dollar Merkle Tree* record identifies **ACM CCS 2026**, with its latest revision still **July 22**. This clarifies publication status; it is not a new September cryptanalytic result. The analysis establishes position binding and extractability for the studied prehashed Plonky3 construction despite properties of its underlying compression function. It does not qualify arbitrary changes to leaves, lengths, compression or transcript framing. [Primary record](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/089).

The **August 22-approved Midpoint Reset preprint** still concerns an original-Poseidon KoalaBear instance whose MDS matrix is chosen after fixed round constants. This recheck found no new revision demonstrating a collision in the pinned native Goldilocks/Poseidon2 instance. Keep the existing requirement for fixed, jointly reviewed parameters; this observation is neither a security proof nor a dismissal of broader attack techniques. [Exact attack model](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/1760).

**New reading: modular proof composition.** Ben Yaacov, Goldreich and Rothblum's *Design Methodologies for Interactive Proof Systems*, published as ECCC TR26-172 on **September 8 at 17:30** (record timezone unspecified), formalizes interactive search reductions and selective message reading, deriving a doubly-efficient PCIP for uniform-NC. Theorem 2.8 accounts for the outer error plus the inner error multiplied by the number of subprotocol calls. Our inference is to use this framework when specifying native proof-stage interfaces and their error accounting. Its interactive theorem does not itself certify our Fiat–Shamir compilation, commitments or 128-bit profile. The record, introduction and selected composition/sumcheck passages were inspected; the full proof was not independently checked. [Primary record](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/172/), [paper, Theorem 2.8 and Sections 3–5](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/172/download).

Two existing qualifications remain decisive. PikkuFold's reported 5.5 KB folding step excludes commitments to fresh inputs; it is not a complete race proof. Action–Orbit's general-input result retains its stated Q2 conjecture, so its proof-size headline cannot justify lowering native FRI queries. Both remain preprints in the inspected records. [PikkuFold, August 29 revision](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/1809), [Action–Orbit and its assumption](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/861).

**13:03 JST follow-up, September 9 (04:03 UTC).** Added Kominers–Thaler–Zheng's *Improved Soundness for the Line–versus–Point Test*, published and revised **August 16**. Theorem 1.1 covers every finite field: local acceptance at least `C(d/q)^(1/3)` implies agreement at least `cε` with a global degree-d polynomial. The stated Lean formalization assumes Lemma 7.1 and Theorem 7.2; no Lean build was run here. [Publication record](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/147/), [revision 1, Section 1.1](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/147/revision/1/download).

This is a Reed–Muller point/affine-line test, rather than our current coset Reed–Solomon proximity protocol. Our inference is to retain it as a candidate theorem for a separately specified construction. Its extension-field coverage does not transfer its bound to native FRI/WHIR or certify fewer queries. Any experiment must first specify the actual oracle domains, sampling, commitments, extraction and Fiat–Shamir composition. No parameter change follows.
