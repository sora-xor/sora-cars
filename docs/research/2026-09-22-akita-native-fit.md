# Akita PCS: native Iroha fit, September 22, 2026

**Recommendation:** retain Akita as a future native polynomial-commitment research candidate. Its linear commitments and setup offloading warrant comparison after an exact arithmetic and opening reduction exists. It is not currently a replacement for our Goldilocks/Fp4 backend. This review changes documentation only; it does not adopt Jolt, a zkVM, dependencies, or a new admitted execution profile.

The [primary preprint](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/1983) was received September 11 and revised September 18. Source observations below use author revision [`252abb895046cc1d5b9955a26a2ad2318148ac26`](https://github.com/LayerZero-Labs/akita/commit/252abb895046cc1d5b9955a26a2ad2318148ac26), committed September 21. That later source revision must not be represented as the exact benchmark artifact. The retrieved PDF SHA-256 is `05a483bd047a76e1dcde1335c7541c1d44a4db9ff346fb6b09ab1e967a51c701`. This is a focused source reading, not an audit, theorem verification, or benchmark reproduction.

**What the paper claims.** Akita is a lattice multilinear PCS based on Module-SIS. Setup offloading authenticates public matrix-prefix work through additional openings. For a fixed integer K ≥ 2, its constant-root schedule family has approximately linear prover work, polylogarithmic proof size, and verifier work proportional to N^(1/K), with polylogarithmic and security-parameter factors hidden. This is not a polylogarithmic-verifier bound. Theorem 9.3 requires regular schedule families; Lemma 9.4 additionally assumes suitable growing SIS parameters. One validated finite schedule does not establish that asymptotic family. [Paper, §§1.1, 9.4](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/1983.pdf).

None is our Goldilocks field, 2^64 − 2^32 + 1. A type named `FpExt4` over another prime cannot represent our Fp4 multiplication by reinterpreting four limbs. The paper's ring/subfield embedding depends on modulus splitting; §3.1 explicitly distinguishes a multiplicative subfield embedding from mere base-linear packing. **Inference:** a Goldilocks port needs a new supported ring/extension construction and parameter analysis, or the complete arithmetic relation must be recompiled over a different field with integer bounds and parity requalified. Generic Rust field traits alone do not resolve this. [Paper, §3.1](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/1983.pdf).

**Transparent setup and trusted policy.** Code derives public matrix prefixes from a fixed public seed using domain-separated SHAKE256 and provides seed/matrix consistency validation. This is reproducible public setup, with no observed secret trapdoor ceremony. Separately, `TrustedScheduleCatalog<Cfg>` binds application-provided artifacts to a concrete configuration. The loader checks the protocol epoch, family, policy digest, row validation and canonical encoding; proof parsing does not install a catalog. Native adoption should freeze the reviewed seed, catalog, field, transcript and policy identities in a compiled profile. A worker must not choose new accepted parameters. [Setup implementation](https://github.com/LayerZero-Labs/akita/blob/252abb895046cc1d5b9955a26a2ad2318148ac26/crates/akita-types/src/proof/setup.rs#L318), [configuration boundary](https://github.com/LayerZero-Labs/akita/blob/252abb895046cc1d5b9955a26a2ad2318148ac26/crates/akita-config/src/lib.rs#L160), [artifact validation](https://github.com/LayerZero-Labs/akita/blob/252abb895046cc1d5b9955a26a2ad2318148ac26/crates/akita-schedules/src/artifact.rs#L210).

**Security qualification.** The author's estimator policy targets 128-bit quantum ADPS16 lattice attack cost. That model is distinct from the paper's classical-ROM knowledge-soundness theorem; a QROM theorem is explicitly future work. Its bound includes `(Q + 1) ε_interactive`, setup error and the relevant Module-SIS advantages. Corollary 10.34 imposes a schedule-specific field-size/query-budget ceiling. Neither a roughly 128-bit extension field nor an estimator flag establishes 128-bit composed security. The exact accepted schedule family, nonce probes, application reductions and hash assumptions need one complete ledger. Model-derived response bounds also leave conditional honest-completeness premises (§12.3). I have not independently verified these reductions or certified any concrete schedule. The repository itself identifies the implementation as unaudited research software. [Estimator policy](https://github.com/LayerZero-Labs/akita/blob/252abb895046cc1d5b9955a26a2ad2318148ac26/crates/akita-sis-estimator/src/config.rs), [paper, §§10–12](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/1983.pdf), [security status](https://github.com/LayerZero-Labs/akita/blob/252abb895046cc1d5b9955a26a2ad2318148ac26/SECURITY.md).

**Measured scope matters.** The abstract's 61–72 KB range covers PCS benchmarks. Table 8's p32 dense workload reports 61.3–67.3 decimal KB opening proofs for 2^22–2^30 coefficients, excluding the 128-byte commitment. At 2^30 coefficients, offloaded Akita reports 24.4 s commitment, 18.4 s opening, 15.9 ms verification and 4.71 GiB peak process RAM. These are author measurements on a Ryzen 9 9950X with AVX-512, single-threaded, medians of ten observations after warmup. Planning/validation precede online timing; reusable setup is accounted separately. Sublinear *additional* prover memory excludes storing the polynomial itself. The paper explicitly cautions that comparison interfaces and security conventions differ. None of these numbers measures a complete race, replay/finality bundle, our resource admission, or Iroha validator cost. [Paper, §13.1–13.2, Table 8, Appendices H–I](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/1983.pdf).

**Integration blockers to clear before an implementation proposal:**

1. Preserve the exact current relation. Our [membership component](/Users/takemiyamakoto/dev/iroha-sora-cars-native/crates/iroha_core/src/execution_proofs/guarded/phased_membership_proof.rs) binds two oracles, all source equations, range/ROM membership, public ports, zero tails, cubic sumcheck and six partial-read claims before joint WHIR openings. Akita needs a proved reduction of those claims and encodings, including field/base-membership obligations; substituting commitment bytes is insufficient.
2. Preserve generic execution bindings and admission. Native statement, sequence/carry, accepted inputs, transcript order, checkpoint/terminal semantics, canonical Norito encoding and immutable profile identity remain obligations. A PCS does not authenticate finality, settle escrow, or aggregate all segment proofs automatically. The [Street adapter](/Users/takemiyamakoto/dev/iroha-sora-cars-native/crates/iroha_core/src/execution_proofs/street_v1/accepted.rs) remains unregistered and unqualified for settlement.
3. Close the security and operational evidence gaps. Review the complete theorem/parameter ledger; bound setup, decoder, proving and verification resources; establish deterministic scalar/accelerated parity on supported Iroha targets; then measure complete 2–8-player traces at each profile's actual cap: Stock 5,400 ticks/180 seconds and Street Tokyo 9,000 ticks/300 seconds at 30 Hz. Source inspection and upstream RSS measurements do not satisfy these gates.
4. Resolve dependency and transcript design deliberately. The pinned code uses Rust 1.95 and `jolt-field` at revision `72dc6451628d8b1dd794147a1f1cc40be0d77963`, plus a spongefish transcript with Blake2b or Keccak. A field-library dependency is not itself a zkVM, but neither it nor a replacement with native Poseidon2 can be imported without a fresh native design and review. [Algebra manifest](https://github.com/LayerZero-Labs/akita/blob/252abb895046cc1d5b9955a26a2ad2318148ac26/crates/akita-algebra/Cargo.toml), [transcript contract](https://github.com/LayerZero-Labs/akita/blob/252abb895046cc1d5b9955a26a2ad2318148ac26/crates/akita-transcript/README.md).

No source-changing experiment, build, production dependency change, or upstream-code execution was performed. The first useful follow-up is a reviewed field-and-opening reduction specification, followed by a separately scoped native comparison with complete cost and security accounting.
