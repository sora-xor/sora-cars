# SORA CARS proof workshop

Visual thesis: a violet CRT workbench in the SORA garage, with pixel cars and luminous arithmetic moving through one large diagram.

Content plan: Japanese title and English subtitle; seven-stage interactive diagram; one focused explanation and hands-on control per stage; native implementation and verification limits below; source papers and a return-to-racing action. No wallet is needed.

Interaction thesis: a car advances through an execution trace; changing a claimed position exposes nonzero transition residuals and changes its commitment; paired polynomial evaluations visibly fold to fewer values. Animation can be paused and follows reduced-motion preferences.

The demonstration uses F17, a deliberately simplified one-car acceleration recurrence, SHA-256 Merkle commitments and public demonstration challenges. It is neither a browser STARK verifier nor a native proof payload. Stock's native execution proof uses the generic bounded-integer engine with Goldilocks/Fp4, domain-separated BLAKE2b-384 commitments, DEEP-FRI and 16-bit range lookups. Its surrounding record and certificate commitments retain their own Poseidon2 domain. The separate Street research path retains its sumcheck/WHIR construction. A field residue alone does not establish bounded integer physics; the native relation must additionally constrain ranges, branches, collisions, checkpoints, inactive slots, padding and outcomes.

The FRI illustration uses f(x)=3+2x+4x²+x³ over the order16 multiplicative group generated by3 in F17. Pair x and −x, then evaluate g(x²)=(f(x)+f(−x))/2 + β(f(x)−f(−x))/(2x). With β=5 this gives g(y)=13+9y. A second fold with β=7 gives constant8 over four evaluation points. This illustrates algebraic folding, not a standalone soundness proof. Random queries, committed rounds, degree bounds, DEEP linking and whole-protocol analysis are separate necessary components.

References: [original FRI paper](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2017/134/) and [DEEP-FRI / DEEP-ALI paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.12243).
