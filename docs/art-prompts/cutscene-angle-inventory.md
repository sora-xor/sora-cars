# Cutscene artwork coverage — September 12, 2026

The campaign has **24 authored cutscenes** (12 intros and 12 aftermaths), one shared three-line retry, **301 dialogue lines**, **229 contiguous scene/shot runs**, and **32 used scene/shot pairs**. `cutscene-angle-inventory.json` records every line, resolved location and shot, speaker, and English dialogue. It uses one-based chapter/line numbers, with the game's zero-based chapter ID included separately.

At audit time, the eight locations each have one image. Every wide/left/right/detail change only crops or pans that same image. All four directions are used in every location, so a complete replacement requires 32 independently composed location-angle images. The emotional events below also need individual artwork because no single neutral scene image can truthfully show both a blackout and a working studio, or both an earlier friendly conversation and the final kiss.

## Continuity

Keep refined 16-bit pixel clusters/dithering, 1986 analog props, violet shadows, amber/ivory light, small physically embedded red SORA insignia. Faces and important props belong above the lower dialogue area. Never add UI or subtitles to paintings. The recurring cassette begins with a blank B-side; the final kiss occurs only after mutual consent. A working SORA X.O.R. prototype is deliberate alternate history, built from period CRTs, boards, radio links and paper receipts.

`left` and `right` are camera directions, not fixed speaker identities: several speakers share each held shot. Compose reverse angles with both conversation partners readable rather than assuming the speaker always stands on the named side.

## Implementation caveats

Current radio reel positions and scene particle masks in `story-cinema.ts` assume the old image composition. Independent art must not inherit object-specific rotations or masks at those old coordinates. Use only spatially safe ambience, or make effects depend on the art asset. Preserve reduced-motion and Back/log shot restoration. Assets should preload only the current dialogue's art set.
